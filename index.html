Melaku Beaulieu

My name is Melaku Beaulieu. I was born in Ethiopia and at a young ages I got adopted and came to America. When I was 3 my mom began to teach me how to paint. I fell in love with it the minute I started, as I found it a way to express. 13 years later I came to LaGuardia High School and began the creation of my portfolio. Here it is.
